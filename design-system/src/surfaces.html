Surfaces

Everything the user touches is glass sitting on a moving map. Four tiers, each with a fixed blur and opacity — the tier tells you how far the surface is from the map, and nothing else varies.

Glass tiers

1 · Chromergba(8,8,8,.82)
blur(16px)
nav, layers, status
2 · Cardrgba(8,8,8,.72)
blur(18px)
panel content
3 · Searchrgba(16,16,20,.6)
blur(24px) sat(180%)
+ inset highlight
4 · Modal#111 — opaque
no blur
over a dimmed scrim

Tier 3 saturates as well as blurs, which is what keeps white input text legible over bright satellite tiles. Tier 4 stops being glass on purpose: once a dialog is open the map is no longer the subject.

Radius

5 · sm
9 · md
11 · lg
18 · xl
pill

11px is the house radius — it is --glass-radius and every chrome surface uses it. Pills are reserved for things that float free of a panel: the search bar, the matched-place chip, the language toggle.

Hairlines & fills

fill 8% / line 14%
fill 4% / line 9%
fill 3% / line 6%

Borders are white at low alpha, never a grey — they have to read against both a dark basemap and a bright one.

Motion

Spring
cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1)
Anything that moves in space — panels, the search bar docking, modal entry.
Ease
ease · 0.15–0.24s
Anything that only changes colour or opacity — hover, focus, fades.
Press
scale(0.97–0.98) · 0.1s
Every button. Nothing else scales.
