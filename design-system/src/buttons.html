Buttons

Solid white is the one primary action on a surface. Violet means Pro. Tinted indigo means “this produces data.” Everything else is quiet.

Hierarchy

One white button per surface, never two. If a panel needs a second affirmative action it becomes tinted indigo, not a second white button.
