__BASE__

Brand

The mark is a location pin drawn in white line on a blue gradient tile — the only place blue appears in the whole product. It always leads; the wordmark follows on its right, never above.

Primary lockup

Wordmark is set at roughly 0.78× the mark height, optically baseline-aligned — not box-aligned. Gap is 10px at a 52px mark, scaling proportionally.

Mark alone

Use the mark alone at 28px and below — in the app header, the browser tab, and any map overlay where the wordmark would not survive the basemap behind it.

In product

App header size: 28px mark / 22px wordmark. Footer drops to 24 / 18.

Rules

DoPlace on the dark canvas, on glass, or on a photographic basemap dark enough to hold white type.
DoKeep clearspace of half the mark height on every side.
Don'tRecolour the mark, add a drop shadow, or set it in the violet accent.
Don'tStack the wordmark under the mark, or letterspace it to fill a width.

Mark colour

#004AAD with a lighter radial highlight toward the base of the pin.
This blue is asset-only. It lives inside the mark and nowhere else — the interface accent is violet. Never sample the mark to colour a button, a link or a map layer.

Endorsement

Urbanyx is a Z.axis product. The Z.axis mark appears once, in the footer only, in white at 60% opacity — never beside the Urbanyx lockup in a header.
