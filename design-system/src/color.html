Colour

Urbanyx is a dark-only product: the interface floats over a live basemap, so every surface is translucent and every colour is chosen to stay legible over satellite imagery, street tiles and terrain alike.

Text ramp

Primary — values, headings, anything the eye lands on first #fff

Secondary — body copy, panel content 72%

Tertiary — placeholders, inactive icons 50%

Quaternary — captions, metadata 35%

Faint — section labels, disabled 28%

Five steps of white, never grey hex values — opacity lets the basemap show through consistently. Text below 35% is decorative and must never be the only carrier of meaning.
