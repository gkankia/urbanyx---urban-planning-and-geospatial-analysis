Urbanyx (urbanyx.zaxis.ge/app) — UX Evaluation, Persona A: The Working Agent

Persona: Nino Beridze, 34, mid-size Tbilisi agency, 7 years, myhome.ge/ss.ge/WhatsApp/Excel user, impatient, Georgian-first. Method: task-based usability evaluation with think-aloud protocol, desktop (~1320px viewport) + narrow-viewport mobile simulation (~280px and ~580px). Session date: 2026-08-17. Account used: logged-in Pro account (pre-existing). Test mode banner active throughout: «სატესტო რეჟიმი. გთხოვთ, არ შეიყვანოთ ბარათის მონაცემები გადახდის პორტალზე! უკუკავშირისთვის, მოგვწერეთ info@zaxis.ge». Layers: persona reactions in quotes ("..."), evaluator observations in plain text.

1. Verdict

Usable by a working agent today: NO for her core money-making tasks; YES-with-caveats as a supporting "know-the-location" tool.

  • The tool contains no prices, no listings, no comparables anywhere that could be located during a full sweep of the UI. Pricing a plot (her task #2 in importance) and building a buyer shortlist are impossible. It solves a planner's problem, not an agent's.
  • What it does — parcel lookup by cadastral code with registry data, street photos, livability/zoning/slope/temperature analysis, K1/K2 build limits, isochrones, AI development concepts, PDF/GeoJSON export, save/restore projects — mostly works, and several outputs (zoning donut, slope stats, LST with date) are better than anything in her current toolkit.
  • Trust is undermined by the product's own behavior: the headline URBAN LIVABILITY INDEX changed value in front of the evaluator without explanation (75 B → 61 C during one load; 9 F → 32 F on a later recompute of the same parcel), and for most numbers neither source nor date is discoverable. Per this persona's brief, that is trust-blocking.
  • Discoverability fails the 20-second rule repeatedly: search accepts only cadastral codes (typing «დიღომი» → «ძიება წარუმატებელია»), the eight analysis buttons are unlabeled icons, POI dots have no legend, and the info popup keeps repositioning so buttons move out from under the cursor.
  • The interface is half Georgian, half English (panel headers, AI outputs, tooltips, dates, empty states), which slows this persona down and looks unfinished to her clients.

3. What works (evidence in brackets)

  1. Cadastral-code lookup is fast and rich — typing the example-format code 01.15.07.002.057 flew to the parcel and returned area, type, address, owner, registration date + 360° street photos [Task 2].
  2. Zoning assessment on a drawn area is a real differentiator — donut of zone shares («საცხოვრებელი ზონა-6 73%») and per-zone tooltips computing K1/K2/K3 into m² («K1=0.50 Max footprint 29,266 m²… Max height (floors) 5») [Task 3/6].
  3. Project save/restore works — «პროექტის შენახვა» stored the drawn area, analyses and POIs; «გახსნა» restored them, with an auto-save chip («Saved just now») [Task 9].
  4. Land-surface-temperature module shows provenance done right — value 21.9°, capture date 2026-07-28, cloudiness 39%, trend slider 2013–2026 [Phase 5 trust audit].
  5. Mobile layout genuinely adapts — bottom tab bar (ნაკვეთი/ანალიზი/პროექტები/პროფილი), bottom-sheet parcel card, double-tap zoom and parcel select all worked at 280px width [Task 10].

6. Structural problems

  • This is a planning/GIS product wearing a map, not an agent tool. Its center of gravity — zoning, coefficients, slope, LST, isochrones, morphology, GeoTIFF import/export, SketchUp export — serves architects, developers, and analysts (the signup form's own sector list starts with «არქიტექტურა / ურბანული დიზაინი»). The agent's daily loop (find listings → price them → convince clients) is untouched: no listings, no prices, no comps, no trends. For Nino it can only ever be a supplement, so its value must be measured against the 2–3 questions a month where zoning/build-potential matters.
  • Coverage boundaries contradict her territory. The pricing table itself states: ზონირების ანალიზი «მხოლოდ თბილისი», რელიეფი «მხოლოდ თბილისი», კლიმატი/სუფთა ენერგია «საქართველო ნაწილობრივ, თბილისი სრულად». Her Mtskheta/Kakheti/Adjara deals fall outside the strongest features, and the tool never says so up front — she'd discover it by silent empty results.
  • The information architecture accumulates instead of organizing. Analysis sections stack vertically in one ever-longer popup (nearby + zoning + donut + compliance + permits + relief + climate…), while unrelated clicks can silently clear them. There is no notion of "my current analysis" she can hold on to, compare, or return to except full project save.
  • The metering model (tokens) is invisible until the account modal. «36 ძიება ამ თვეში / 964 დარჩენილი / თვიური ლიმიტი: 1,000» — nothing in the working UI shows that actions consume a budget; a Free user (50 parcel analyses/month) would hit the wall without warning.
  • Anchor mismatch for the buyer-shortlist job: isochrones are computed from the selected parcel/area, and travel-time options are 10/15/20/45/60 min. "Within 25 minutes of Vake" (from a point to candidate areas, at 25 min) cannot be expressed; there is no way to enumerate candidate parcels by criteria (size range, buildability) at all.

12. Questions for the product team

  1. Who is the primary user? The signup sector list says architects/planners/developers first — is the real-estate agent a target persona at all, or an accidental visitor?
  2. The livability index changes value during load and between recomputations of the same object (75 B→61 C; 9 F→32 F). Is the score deterministic? What are the component weights?
  3. What exactly does «სანდოობა: E · 46%» measure — transit punctuality, data completeness, or model confidence?
  4. What are the sources and update dates for: registry parcels, transit stops/routes, parking, POI categories, zoning polygons, DEM? Are you allowed to display private owners' names?
  5. Is place-name/address geocoding on the roadmap for search, and when?
  6. Zoning is «მხოლოდ თბილისი» — what's the expansion plan (Batumi, Mtskheta at minimum for this persona)?
  7. Why is the AI concept output English when the prompt is Georgian?
  8. Is the PDF report intended as a client-facing deliverable (branding, language, disclaimer) or an internal dump? Can it get a shareable link instead of a file?
  9. Do drawn areas/analyses count against the 1,000 token quota? Where is that shown to the user before they hit the limit?
