Urbanyx (urbanyx.zaxis.ge/app) — UX & Rigour Evaluation, Persona C: The Power User

Persona: Salome Kapanadze, 29. Land acquisition & analysis at a Tbilisi developer building 4–9 storey residential. Economics degree, lives in Excel, some SQL, a third of a QGIS course. Reads cadastral extracts, talks to architects weekly, knows k1/k2 and what a განაშენიანების რეგულირების გეგმა constrains. Her job: is this parcel worth acquiring, and at what price? Her frustration is shallowness, not difficulty.

Method: task-based usability and rigour evaluation with think-aloud protocol. Session 2026-08-18/19 (Tbilisi), viewport 859×939 CSS px, signed in on a pre-existing Pro account. Numbers in this report were extracted from the live DOM or read at ≥2× magnification, not transcribed from thumbnails. Network hosts were read from the browser's own request log (passive observation of requests the page made — no endpoint was called by the evaluator). Every inference is labelled [inferred].

Layers: persona reactions in quotes ("..."), evaluator observations in plain text.

1. Verdict

Usable for developer-side land analysis today: no — as an analysis tool. Yes-with-caveats as a fast zoning-and-registry lookup.

  • The zoning module is the product. It is deterministic, internally consistent and arithmetically correct: four zones hand-checked, every K1/K2/K3 product reconciles to the displayed m², and zone areas sum to the site area (48,100 vs 48,101 m²). Re-run from a fresh session on restored geometry, all values were identical.
  • The flagship "URBAN LIVABILITY INDEX" is not a property of the site. Identical geometry, same session: 71 B with nearby analysis alone, 80 A after switching on «მისაწვდომობის ზონა» (15-min walk isochrone) — because the catchment silently changes from the site to a ~1.5 km walking zone and the component set changes from three to four. Nothing on the score card states the catchment. Any figure she quotes from it is unfalsifiable without her toggle state.
  • Nulls are rendered as zeros in numbers she would model. Three of four zones report «K2=0.00 → Max floor area 0 m² · Max height (floors) 0» while simultaneously allowing a footprint (სსზ-3: K1=0.70 → 7,577 m²). "No data" and "building prohibited" are indistinguishable on screen.
  • There is no price, no comparable, no transaction anywhere — a DOM scan for 14 price/market terms (ფასი, ლარი, ₾, GEL, USD, ღირებულებ, საბაზრო, price, valuation, market value, ტრანზაქც, გარიგებ, comparable, ანალოგ) returned zero hits. Tasks 3 and 9 (comparables, price trend) have no surface to test.
  • No methodology, no provenance, no freshness, no API, no CRS — absent in the app and absent on the public site. The only data source she can name is one the tool disclosed by failing: a toast reading «maps.gov.ge მიუწვდომელია — სცადეთ მოგვიანებით...».

2. Rigour verdict

Arithmetic: passes. Definitions: fails.

The hand-check (Task 4) reconciles exactly. For a 4.81 ha drawn site in Dighomi Massive, «საცხოვრებელი ზონა-6» reports Zone area 24,759 m², and 24,759 × 0.50 = 12,379.5 → displayed 12,379 m²; × 2.50 = 61,897.5 → 61,897 m²; × 0.30 = 7,427.7 → 7,428 m²; K2÷K1 = 2.50/0.50 → 5 floors. All four zone tooltips reconcile the same way, and the four zone areas (24,759 + 10,825 + 6,669 + 5,847 = 48,100 m²) match the site area shown in the compliance block (48,101 m²). Reproducibility (Task 5) is clean for this module: project saved, browser reloaded, project reopened, zoning re-run — every figure identical, to the digit.

The failure is upstream of the arithmetic, in what the numbers mean. The same 4.81 ha site scores 71 B or 80 A depending on an unrelated toggle, with the count basis changing underneath (stops 6 → 34, parking 20 → 80, kindergartens 1 → 6) and the component list shrinking from four to three when a data source fails — signalled only by a word vanishing from a 9-px subtitle and a tip that reads «მიწათსარგებლობის (მრავალფეროვნების) მონაცემი ვერ ჩაიტვირთა». A composite index published with a silently missing component, on an undisclosed catchment, is not defensible to a CFO. "ციფრი, რომელიც შვიდ ქულით იცვლება იმის მიხედვით, რომელი ღილაკი მაქვს ჩართული, ჩემს მოდელში ვერ შევა."

Separately, the geocoder is not deterministic across sessions: the identical query «დევი აბაშიძის ქუჩა თბილისი» resolved to Dighomi Massive (44.7797, 41.7641) in one session and to the Ortachala/Navtlughi side of the city (44.8377, 41.6874) in another — ~9 km apart, no matched-place label either time. Proximity biasing from the current map centre is the likely mechanism [inferred]; the effect is that a saved query is not a saved location.

3. Ceiling statement

The ceiling is the point where a number has to be defended or combined. She can get, in about a minute per site: the cadastral code, area, use type, address string, owner string, registration date, the zone mix of a drawn site, and each zone's K1/K2/K3 turned into m² of footprint, floor area, floors and required greening — plus slope statistics, an elevation profile, land-surface temperature with a capture date, and a walking isochrone. That is a real acceleration of the first hour of due diligence. Everything after that is manual: there is no netting of unbuildable zones into a single site capacity, no setbacks or building lines, no deduction of existing structures, no parcel list or owner count under a drawn area (so no assembly feasibility), no attribute query to build a shortlist, no price or comparable of any kind, no confidence interval on anything, no source or date to cite, and no API or auditable export to move data into her model. So she leaves the tool at exactly the moment her actual question starts — what can we build, sell, and pay? — and reopens Excel for the capacity stack, the registry (napr.gov.ge) for encumbrances and owner detail, myhome.ge/ss.ge and her own transaction notes for pricing, and QGIS for anything geometric she needs to keep.

5. What works

  1. Zoning coefficients converted to m² per zone, with the intersected zone area shown — hand-checked exact on four zones (§2), and the four zone areas sum to the site area.
  2. Deterministic and restorable: project saved → reload → reopen → re-run produced byte-identical zoning output; drawn geometry restored exactly (4.81 ha, 902 m, 278 m × 174 m).
  3. Zoning survived the upstream registry outage — parcel clicks were dead («maps.gov.ge მიუწვდომელია») while zoning, slope and area analysis on drawn geometry kept working [inferred: zoning is not served from maps.gov.ge].
  4. Slope module is the only one that states its basis: «წყარო: თბილისის მაღალი გარჩევადობის ციფრული რელიეფის მოდელი. მნიშვნელობები ინდიკატიურია და არ ცვლის საველე აზომვას».
  5. Rural cadastral coverage is real (Kakheti parcel 53.03.34.014, 7,633 კვ.მ., «სასოფლო-სამეურნეო», named owner, 2011 registration), so the screening step is not Tbilisi-only even though most analytics are.

12. Structural problems

  • The product models "place quality", she models "residual land value". Its centre of gravity is an amenity index and a concept generator; hers is a capacity stack (site → zoning → net buildable → sellable m² → revenue → land price). The two only touch at the zoning module, which is the one piece she would pay for.
  • Analysis is presented as display. The livability card, the donut and the POI dots look like analysis but expose no parameters and cannot be re-based, filtered or exported. The one genuinely analytical output (zoning coefficients) is buried in hover tooltips — the least citable, least exportable surface in a UI.
  • Nothing is addressable, so nothing is auditable. No URL state, no analysis IDs, no run timestamps, no source/as-of fields. Two colleagues cannot verify they are looking at the same computation, which for a procurement decision is disqualifying independent of data quality.
  • The tool is a thin client over the public registry portal, without owning the risk. Parcel data comes from maps.gov.ge live; when that portal is down the core function dies (sometimes loudly, sometimes silently), and there is no cache, no last-known-good timestamp, and no statement in the product that this dependency exists.
  • Metering is invisible while the unit of value is a "search". Quota lives in rpc/get_search_count and surfaces only in the account modal («თვიური ლიმიტი: 1,000»); a Free account's 50 analyses/month is not shown where the analysis is run. For a team buying seats, cost per screened parcel is not observable in the product.
